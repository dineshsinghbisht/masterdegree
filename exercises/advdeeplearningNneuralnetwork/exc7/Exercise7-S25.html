Explain the above figure?

Answer here!!!

The figure shows the accuracy of the model on the training and test datasets during 1000 epochs.

At the beginning both accuracies rise quickly close to .80 but After that the training accuracy keeps going up and stays around 0.93 to 0.95, but the test accuracy goes down a little and stays around 0.86 to 0.87. The two lines separate and do not come back together. This is overfitting as the model learns the training data, including its noise, but does not work as well on new data ie test data.

The reason is that the network is very large (500 hidden nodes) and the training set is very small (only 125 samples). The model has enough freedom to memorize the training points instead of learning the general shape of the two moons.

The lines are also very jagged. This is probably because the learning rate (0.1) is high, so the weights change a lot at every step.

Conclusion¶

NOTE: Conclusion is one evaluation criterion for the exercise, so pay attention to writing them

Write conclusion about results of used regularization methods

The initial simple NN model (baseline model) resulted in overfitting. All four regularization methods solved this main problem, the test accuracy no longer went down while the training accuracy went up. L2 and Dropout gave the best results, while L1 and L1+L2 restricted the model too much, which caused a little underfitting.

Baseline (no regularization): The model overfitted. At the beginning, the training and test accuracy went up together. After that, the training accuracy kept going up while the test accuracy slowly went down. The big gap between the two lines shows that the model memorized the training data, including its noise. This was expected, because the network is very large and the training set is very small.

L2 regularization: L2 removed the overfitting. The training and test accuracy stayed close together for the whole training, and the test accuracy no longer went down. The training accuracy is lower than in the baseline, because the model can no longer memorize the training points. L2 gave a smooth and stable result.

L1 regularization: L1 also removed the overfitting, because the two lines stay together. But both accuracies are lower than with L2, so the penalty was probably too strong for this model, and the model underfits a little. A different strength might give a better result.

L1 and L2 together: The result is almost the same as with L1 alone. The overfitting is gone, but the accuracy is lower than with L2 alone. It looks like the L1 part has the strongest effect, so adding L2 did not change much.

Dropout: Dropout also removed the overfitting. The test accuracy stayed high and stable during the whole training. The training line is noisy and a bit below the test line. This is because dropout switches off part of the nodes during training, but not during testing, so the training accuracy looks worse than it really is.
